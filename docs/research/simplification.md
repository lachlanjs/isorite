# Simplifying isorite: an audit

Read-only audit, 2026-11-09, at commit d29f33e (0.21.6). Nothing was edited except this file. Evidence is `file:line` plus the grep that shows there is nothing left pointing at it. Sizes are bytes of tracked text unless stated. knip and ts-prune are not installed and `npx` could not fetch them offline, so dead exports were found with a script: for every `export` in `packages/*/src` and `app/src`, a word-match grep over every tracked `.ts/.js/.mjs/.svelte/.cjs` outside `inspiration/`. Dead CSS in `app/src/app.css` was checked the same way and is clean (the only unreferenced selectors are KaTeX's own). CSS inside the 45 `<style>` blocks of the `.svelte` files was not checked.

## 0. What the repository is made of

741 tracked files. By weight of text: `packages/server` 612 KB (92 files, 38 of them tests), `packages/core` 329 KB, `packages/engine` 223 KB, `kit` 136 KB, `app` about 670 KB of source (45 components, 414 KB), `docs/` 807 KB, `fixtures/` 238 KB, `bench/` 176 KB, `inspiration/` 158 KB, `eval/` 56 KB. By weight of binaries tracked: `tern-recordings/` 18.7 MB (four mp4s that nothing references), `thom-pool-mtv.gif` 369 KB, the root PDF 166 KB, `screenshots/` 495 KB. Nine more screenshots are untracked.

The shape of the problem: the code is mostly sound and dense, with little copy-paste. The redundancy is of three other kinds. First, whole subsystems that the M25 retirement of Iso's own loop left behind and that nothing feeds any more (proposals, the learner record, format-1 study bases, Iso conversations' tier and mode). Second, a large pile of spikes, sketches and evals with no runner. Third, a vocabulary that has grown faster than the product: about 45 nouns, several for one idea, with "Iso" now naming a pane, a gold diamond, a retired model and 245 places in code.

## 3. Docs: archive and condense (about 807 KB now)

Counts: 33 `docs/plan-m*.md` files, 588 KB (73 percent of docs). Biggest: `plan-m36` 44 KB, `plan-m6` 43 KB, `plan-m21` 32 KB, `plan-m23` 29 KB, `plan-m25` 27 KB, `plan-m15` 27 KB. 2. Write `docs/history.md` (target 10 to 15 KB): one row per milestone, M1 to M37: the date, the one-sentence outcome, what survived, what was retired and by which milestone, and the archive link. This is the only thing a newcomer or agent reads about the past.
3. Archive `tui-research.md` and the retired sections of `architecture.md` and `ISORITE_CONTEXT.md` (move whole sections to `docs/archive/architecture-retired.md` and `context-predecessor.md`). Rewrite what remains of `architecture.md` as "what is built now" (about 12 KB, down from 27.6 KB): layers, ports (only `Files` and `Clock` exist; `engine/src/ports.ts` has no Http or Secrets, yet `architecture.md:237` still names `MemorySecrets` and a scripted `Http`), the note's life, search, the Atlas, events, security, testing.
4. Fold `ISORITE_CONTEXT.md` §3 to §4 (decisions that stand, style) into `decisions.md` and `taste.md` (the style is already `taste.md`, 12 KB; check overlap with CONTEXT §4's 9 KB), and archive the remainder.
5. Keep live: `README`, `format.md` (14 KB, the contract), `decisions.md` (16 KB), `taste.md`, `ISORITE_DOC.md` (30 KB, the vision, but see concept decisions: it describes seeds, study mode and the TUI as designs), `release.md`, `ideas.md`, `atlas-layout.md`, and one setup document (R12). ## 5. Concept decisions the owner must make

1. Is "Iso" the agent's name or not? The product already says "Agent" in the UI, the README, the guide's setup notes, and the retired model had the name. The component and file names, `notes/iso/` (9 notes), the gold diamond and 245 code mentions still say Iso. Either rename everything to Agent (the diamond stays as a logo) or give Iso a clear meaning ("the agent isorite hosts") and stop using it for other agents. This blocks R6 and the guide's structure.
2. How many ways in? Four are supported (own agent through pi, one in the pane through ACP, `isorite acp` for front ends, MCP in the agent's own terminal) and the PDF in the repo root compared three. Each has its own code (`pi/` 7 files, `acp.ts` + `acp-wrap.ts` 65 KB, `mcp*.ts`, `connect.ts`, `setup/harness.ts`), settings and docs. The leanest honest set is MCP (any agent, nothing to host) plus one in-pane route. Decide which one.
3. Merge questions and problems into one kind, and the tray into pointers? These two decisions remove the most nouns for the least code: one note kind, one way to send things to the agent.
