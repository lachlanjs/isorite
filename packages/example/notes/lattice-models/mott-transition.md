---
type: Concept
title: Mott transition
description: A metal that becomes an insulator because of interactions, not because of band filling.
---

At half filling the [Hubbard model](/lattice-models/hubbard-model.md "requires") has a half-filled band, so band theory says metal. For $U \gg W$ it is an insulator anyway: moving an electron onto an occupied neighbour costs $U$.

Somewhere around $U \sim W$ it changes. Coming from the metal, the [quasiparticle weight](/many-body-basics/quasiparticle-weight.md "see also") goes to zero; coming from the insulator, a gap of order $U - W$ closes.
