---
type: Concept
title: Lattice to impurity map
description: The lattice problem is replaced by an impurity problem with the same local physics.
---

The single-site action from the cavity construction is the action of an [Anderson impurity model](/impurity-problems/anderson-impurity-model.md "requires") with a particular bath. So:

1. Pick a bath, that is, a hybridisation function.
2. Solve the impurity: get $G_{\text{imp}}$ and $\Sigma_{\text{imp}}$.
3. Use $\Sigma_{\text{imp}}$ as the lattice's [local self-energy](/dmft/local-self-energy.md "requires").

The map is exact in infinite dimensions and an approximation in finite ones. What it cannot capture: anything that needs $\Sigma$ to depend on $k$.
