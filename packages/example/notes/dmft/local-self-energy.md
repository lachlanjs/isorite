---
type: Concept
title: Local self-energy
description: In infinite dimensions the self-energy depends on frequency only.
---

Each line between two different sites carries a factor $t \sim z^{-1/2}$. In [infinite dimensions](/dmft/infinite-dimensions.md "requires") every skeleton diagram for the [self-energy](/many-body-basics/self-energy.md "requires") between different sites has more such factors than there are neighbours to sum over, so it vanishes:

$$
\Sigma_{ij}(\omega) = \delta_{ij}\, \Sigma(\omega), \qquad \Sigma(k, \omega) = \Sigma(\omega).
$$

The self-energy keeps its full frequency dependence: the dynamics stay. Only the spatial correlations go. This is what "dynamical mean field" means.
