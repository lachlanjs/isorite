---
type: Concept
title: Infinite dimensions
description: The limit in which the self-energy stops depending on momentum.
---

Metzner and Vollhardt (1989): take the [Hubbard model](/lattice-models/hubbard-model.md "requires") on a lattice with $z$ neighbours and let $z \to \infty$. For the kinetic energy to stay finite the hopping has to be scaled,

$$
t = \frac{t^*}{\sqrt{z}}.
$$

With that scaling the interaction and the kinetic energy stay in competition, so the limit is not trivial, but many diagrams vanish.

The payoff: the self-energy becomes local.
