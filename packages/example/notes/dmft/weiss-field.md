---
type: Concept
title: Weiss field
description: The bare propagator of the single-site problem.
---

The Weiss field $\mathcal{G}_0(i\omega_n)$ is the bare propagator of the single-site problem: what an electron on the site sees before its own interactions are counted. It is built from the [lattice to impurity map](/dmft/lattice-to-impurity-map.md "requires") and the [hybridisation function](/impurity-problems/hybridisation-function.md "requires"):

$$
\mathcal{G}_0^{-1}(i\omega_n) = i\omega_n + \mu - \Delta(i\omega_n)
$$

By analogy with the mean field of magnetism. There the field is one number. Here it depends on frequency.

It is not known beforehand. It is found together with the self-energy, in the DMFT loop.
