---
type: Concept
title: Units
description: G = 1, and the total mass and size of order one.
---

Simulations use $G = 1$ and choose the unit of mass and of length so that the numbers are of order one. The usual choice (Hénon's units): total mass $M = 1$, and total energy $E = -1/4$, which makes the system's size about 1 and its crossing time about 2.

Converting back is one choice of scale: say the unit of mass is $10^{11}$ suns and the unit of length 10 kpc; then the unit of time follows from $G = 1$.
