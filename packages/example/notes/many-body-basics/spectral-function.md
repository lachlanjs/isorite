---
type: Concept
title: Spectral function
description: The probability of adding or removing an electron at a given energy.
---

$$
A(k,\omega) = -\frac{1}{\pi}\operatorname{Im} G^R(k,\omega)
$$

It is positive and integrates to one over $\omega$, so it reads as a distribution of energies for an electron of momentum $k$. Photoemission measures the part below the Fermi level.

Free electrons: a delta function at $\varepsilon_k$. With interactions it spreads, into a quasiparticle peak plus incoherent weight elsewhere, often the Hubbard bands.

The local version, $A(\omega) = \sum_k A(k,\omega)$, is the density of states of the interacting system. It comes from the [Green's function](/many-body-basics/greens-function.md "requires").
