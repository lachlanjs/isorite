---
type: Concept
title: Centre of mass
description: A group of bodies seen from far away pulls like one body of their total mass, at their centre of mass.
---

For bodies of masses $m_i$ at $\mathbf{r}_i$:

$$
M = \sum_i m_i, \qquad \mathbf{R} = \frac{1}{M} \sum_i m_i \mathbf{r}_i
$$

Seen from a distance $d$ much larger than the group's size $s$, its pull is that of a single mass $M$ at $\mathbf{R}$, with an error of order $(s/d)^2$.

That is the whole idea behind tree codes: far groups are summed once.
