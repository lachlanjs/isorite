---
type: Concept
title: The Gauss equation
description: K written in E, F, G and their derivatives.
---

In a chart with $F = 0$ (an orthogonal chart),

$$K = -\frac{1}{2\sqrt{EG}} \left[ \frac{\partial}{\partial u}\!\left( \frac{G_u}{\sqrt{EG}} \right) + \frac{\partial}{\partial v}\!\left( \frac{E_v}{\sqrt{EG}} \right) \right].$$

For general $F$ the expression is longer, but it is built the same way: second derivatives of $E$, $F$, $G$ and products of first derivatives, over $EG - F^2$. No $e$, $f$ or $g$ appears.

This is what proves the Theorema Egregium: $K$ is read off $E$, $F$ and $G$.
