title: Theorema Egregium
description: K depends only on the first fundamental form.

**Gauss's remarkable theorem:** the Gaussian curvature $K$, defined through the second fundamental form and the way the surface sits in space, depends only on $E$, $F$, $G$ and their derivatives. It can be found by creatures who never leave the surface.

It follows from the [first fundamental form](/surfaces/first-ff.md "requires") through the [Gauss equation](/curvature/gauss-equation.md "requires"), which writes $K$ in $E$, $F$, $G$ alone.

One consequence: a sphere cannot be flattened onto a plane without stretching, because $K$ differs, and no map that keeps lengths can change $K$. Which is why every flat map of the Earth lies.

Gauss's own account is in his [Disquisitiones generales, 1827](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disquisitiones_generales_circa_superficies_curvas).
