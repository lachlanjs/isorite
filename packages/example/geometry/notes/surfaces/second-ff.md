---
type: Concept
title: The second fundamental form
description: "II = e du² + 2f du dv + g dv²: how the surface bends away from its tangent plane."
---

With a unit normal $N$ chosen, the second fundamental form of a chart is

$$II = e\,du^2 + 2f\,du\,dv + g\,dv^2, \qquad e = \mathbf{x}_{uu} \cdot N,\; f = \mathbf{x}_{uv} \cdot N,\; g = \mathbf{x}_{vv} \cdot N.$$

It measures how far the surface leaves its tangent plane, to second order. It needs the surface to sit in space, which the [first fundamental form](/surfaces/first-ff.md "see also") does not.

Turning $N$ to $-N$ changes the sign of $II$. The [regular surface](/surfaces/regular-surfaces.md "requires") must be smooth enough to have second derivatives.
