---
type: Concept
title: Orientable surfaces
description: A continuous choice of unit normal; the Möbius band has none.
---

A [regular surface](/surfaces/regular-surfaces.md "requires") is **orientable** if it has a continuous choice of unit normal $N$ over the whole of it. The sphere, the torus and every graph are orientable.

The Möbius band is not: carry a unit normal once round the core of the band and it comes back as $-N$. So there is no $N$ to choose, and the sign of $II$ cannot be fixed.

Orientation is a global matter. Locally, every surface has two choices of normal.
