---
type: Concept
title: Triangulations
description: Every compact surface can be cut into finitely many triangles.
---

A **triangulation** of a surface is a finite family of triangles, each a curved triangle in a chart, that cover it, and meet only along whole edges or at whole vertices.

Every compact regular surface has one. This is what makes global statements possible: it turns a surface into a finite list of faces, edges and vertices that can be counted.

Many triangulations exist for one surface, and they differ in how many triangles they use.
