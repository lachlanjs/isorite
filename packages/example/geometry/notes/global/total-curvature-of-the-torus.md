What is $\iint_T K\,dA$ for a torus $T$, whatever its shape?

## Solution

A torus is compact and oriented with no boundary, so the boundary and corner terms vanish.

$\chi(T) = 0$.

So $\iint_T K\,dA = 2\pi\chi(T) = 0$.
