---
type: Concept
title: Euler characteristic
description: χ = V − E + F, the same for every triangulation.
---

For a [triangulation](/global/triangulations.md "requires") of a compact surface with $V$ vertices, $E$ edges and $F$ faces,

$$\chi = V - E + F.$$

It is the same for every triangulation of the surface, so it belongs to the surface itself: $\chi = 2$ for the sphere, $0$ for the torus, and $2 - 2g$ for a surface of genus $g$.
