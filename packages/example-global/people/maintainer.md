---
type: Concept
title: The maintainer
tags: [people, role]
description: Who merges, and who to ask when a convention is unclear.
---

The maintainer owns what lands on the main branch and what the conventions say. A question about [naming](/conventions/naming.md "see also") or [commit messages](/conventions/commit-messages.md "see also") goes to them first, and the answer goes into the convention, so it is asked once.
