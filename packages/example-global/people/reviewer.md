---
type: Concept
title: The reviewer
tags: [people, role]
description: Who reads a change before it lands, and what they are owed.
---

A reviewer is owed a change they can read in one sitting, with a message that says why. They are not owed a defence: if a thing needed explaining, the [notes](/conventions/writing-notes.md "see also") or the code should have said so. The [maintainer](/people/maintainer.md "see also") settles a disagreement between reviewers.
