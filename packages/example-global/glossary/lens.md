---
type: Concept
title: Lens
tags: [glossary]
description: A named, declared way of grouping a base's notes into islands.
---

A lens is a file in `.isorite/lenses/` that groups a [base's](/glossary/base.md "see also") notes into [islands](/glossary/island.md "see also") by listing them or by rules on kind, tag, path and links. The folders are the lens you always have; the others are views on top of it.
