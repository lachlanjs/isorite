type: Concept
title: Problems
description: A problem is something to do, and you say when it is solved.
---

A problem is a note of its own, `type: Problem`, that uses the notes it names in its details: an exercise to try, a calculation to do, a thing to build. Like [a question](/questions-and-problems/questions.md "uses"), it stands beside those notes on the Atlas.

You mark it solved, with the ✓ beside its title, which adds `solved:` and the date. Nothing checks it but you. Write your solution in the problem's note, under a heading, if you want it kept.

Given a problem, [Iso](/iso/iso.md "see also") checks your working rather than solving it: it says where a step goes wrong, and leaves the next step to you.

The things to do in this guide are problems, and [Start here](/start-here.md "see also") holds the first.
