---
type: Concept
title: How the agent reads you
tags: [questions, agent]
description: Your agent reads where you stand from your own notes, questions and problems.
---

isorite keeps no score and no record of you apart from your notes, and [your agent](/agent/agent.md "requires") is asked to read you from them. Where you stand is in what you wrote: your words in a note, your [questions](/questions-and-problems/questions.md "uses") open and resolved, the [problems](/questions-and-problems/problems.md "uses") you have solved and not.

Asked to explain, it is to read those first, and ask where you stand before it explains. Where it finds your understanding stops, it offers that to you as a question to keep, or a problem to try, so the place is marked in your base and not only in the conversation.
