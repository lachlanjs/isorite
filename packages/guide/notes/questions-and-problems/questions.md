---
type: Concept
title: Questions
description: An open question marks where your understanding stops.
---

A question is a note of its own, `type: Question`, about the notes it names in its details:

```markdown
---
type: Question
title: Why does leapfrog not drift?
about: [integrators/leapfrog]
---
```

It is where your understanding stops, written down, so that it is not lost: on the Atlas beside the notes it is about, until you resolve it, by writing what you found and adding `resolved:` with the date. Then it leaves the map.

A note can hold a question in place, on a line of its own, by the question's file name:

```markdown
[[!why-does-leapfrog-not-drift]]
```

Make one from the Atlas, holding on ground and choosing a question, or from the note's settings. [Iso](/iso/iso.md "see also") may ask you one back, and you choose whether to keep it.

A question is the start of [a problem](/questions-and-problems/problems.md "see also"), sometimes.
