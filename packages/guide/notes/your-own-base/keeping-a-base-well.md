title: Keeping a base well
description: One idea a note, linked by what it rests on, in your own words.
---

What isorite asks of a base, and what [Iso](/iso/iso.md "uses") is told to keep to:

- **One idea a note**, with a title that names the idea and a description that says it in a line.
- **Look before you write.** If a note says it already, extend that note.
- **Link by what it rests on**: "requires" for what must be understood first, "uses" for what it draws on. See [links and their kinds](/notes/links-and-their-kinds.md "requires").
- **In your own words.** Iso puts no words in your notes you did not ask for, and its own stay marked until you make them yours.
- **Leave an open question open.** It marks where your understanding stops: see [questions](/questions-and-problems/questions.md "uses").
- **Check, do not solve.** A problem is yours to solve; Iso checks your solution.

A note that holds two ideas, or two notes that hold one, are yours to split or merge.
