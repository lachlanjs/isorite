title: Send the agent an image

Paste a picture into the box in the [Agent pane](/agent/agent.md "uses"), or drop one on the pane. It stands above the box as a small thumbnail, which you can take out with its cross or, from the keyboard, by focusing the cross and pressing Enter (Delete on it does the same). It goes with the message you send, and stands in the session under what you asked, still there after a reload.

Only some models see pictures. Where the model chosen does not, the thumbnail says so, and the message goes without it. A picture must be a png, jpeg, gif or webp, and no more than 5 MB; one that is not says why on its thumbnail and is not sent. Up to six go with a message, and not while the agent is working: wait for it to stop.

The picture is kept apart from the session, by what it holds, in isorite's own data folder (not in your base), so a long session of screenshots stays small. When a long session is shortened to make room, the summary notes that there was a picture and does not carry it. This is the way to show the agent a picture; one in a note is read by its words, not by its pixels. To keep a picture in the base, [drop it on a note](/notes/diagrams-and-code.md "see also").
