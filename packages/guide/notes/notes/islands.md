A base is a folder of notes, and may hold folders inside it, which the Atlas draws as islands, as this guide does: one for notes, one for [the Atlas](/atlas/the-atlas.md "see also"), one for the agent, and so on.

An island is a frame on the Atlas, open when it is big enough on the screen to show its notes, and one block when not. Click it to bring it to the screen; click it again to step out. Links between notes in different islands are drawn between the islands, along the gaps of the map.

To make a note, hold still on open ground on the Atlas, inside the island it belongs in, and let go: "Untitled note" is made where you held, open to type its title over. Hold and drag a little way to make an island instead. A note can also be moved by changing its island in its details.
