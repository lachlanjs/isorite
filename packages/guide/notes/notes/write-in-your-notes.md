Open [your notes](/your-notes.md) and write a line of your own in it, with one word in **bold** and a formula between dollars.

Click away from the line, and see it as it reads.

The text is sized in the browser's own units: zoom the page, or raise the browser's font size, and the notes wrap inside their pane at the size you chose.
