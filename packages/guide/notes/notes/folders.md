type: Concept
title: Folders
description: A base's folders are its parts, and frames on the Atlas.
---

A base is a folder of notes, and may hold folders, as this guide does: one for notes, one for [the Atlas](/atlas/the-atlas.md "see also"), one for Iso, and so on.

A folder is a frame on the Atlas: click it to close it to one block, and again to open it in place. Links between notes in different folders are drawn between the folders, along the gaps of the map.

To make a note, hold still on open ground on the Atlas, inside the folder it belongs in, and let go: "Untitled note" is made where you held, open to type its title over. Hold and drag a little way to make a folder instead. A note can also be moved by changing its folder in its details.
