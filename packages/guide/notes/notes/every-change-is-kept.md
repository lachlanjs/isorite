---
type: Concept
title: Every change is kept
tags: [notes, history]
description: Every change to a note is kept, with who made it.
---

isorite keeps every change to every note in a journal, in `.isorite/`, with who made it: you, or an agent you attached (or Iso, before isorite attached your own).

Two switches in the Note pane's header show it:

- **The timeline** (Alt+T): the note's history as layers in its margin, the newest on top, each as thick as the change was large: yours blue, an agent's gold. Click one to see the note as it was then, and restore it to there if you want it back. Restoring is itself a change, so nothing is lost.
- **Provenance** (Alt+P): whose words are whose. Words you wrote are plain; words an agent wrote, and you have not touched, are marked. Edit them, and they become yours.

This is why [Iso writes, marked](/iso/iso-writes-marked.md "see also"), and why letting it write is safe.
