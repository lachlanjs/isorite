title: A note's details
description: A note's type, title, description and tags, at its top.
---

A note starts with its details, between two lines of `---`:

```markdown
---
type: Concept
title: Leapfrog
description: A step that keeps energy bounded.
tags: [integrators]
---
```

- **type** is what the note is: a `Concept`, the usual; a `Question` (see [questions](/questions-and-problems/questions.md "see also")); or a `Problem` (see [problems](/questions-and-problems/problems.md "see also")).
- **title** is its name, on the Atlas and in links. The file's name is apart from it.
- **description** says the idea in a line. Iso reads it first, when it looks through the base, so a good one helps it find the note.
- **tags**, if you want them.

The Note pane shows them in its label, under the title, and changes them there: the description is the italic line, the kind is one of three, the tags are in brackets, and pressing the folder or the name moves or renames the file. Deleting a note is in the Note pane's menu.
