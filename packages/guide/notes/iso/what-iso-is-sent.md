---
type: Concept
title: What Iso is sent
description: What goes with each turn, part by part, each yours to switch.
---

Each turn, [Iso](/iso/iso.md "requires") is sent what is on in its context, and nothing else. The gauge in Iso's header shows how much of the model's room that is; click it for the Context view, a row for each part with its size, in three groups:

- **Notes.** What you gave it, and what it read.
  - *Given by you*: a note, a folder, or a passage. Shift+click a note on the Atlas, or hold on it, or drag it to Iso's pane; select words in a note and choose "send to Iso". A given part stays until you take it out (✕).
  - *Read by Iso*: a note it read stays in hand for the turns after, so it is not read again. One it has not used for three turns is set aside, and Iso is told it can read it again. Click a row to set it aside or bring it back.
- **The conversation.** Each earlier turn, sent whole. When there are many, sum them up into a briefing, which is sent instead of them; or start a new conversation from a briefing of this one.
- **Always sent.** What Iso is told, the base's [AGENTS.md](/iso/agents-md.md "see also"), and your message.

Click a row to switch it off or on. Nothing is cut without your saying so: a turn too big for the model is not sent, and you choose what to leave out. Only when a turn would not fit is the conversation summed up on its own.

On the Atlas, Iso's context switch (Alt+C) marks what is in it.
