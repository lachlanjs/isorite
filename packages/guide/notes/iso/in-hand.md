---

The gauge in Iso's header shows how full the agent's context is, as the agent says. Click it for the Context view: a column in layers, as wide as each part's share of what the model takes.

- **isorite's**: its instructions and its tools, as isorite gives them to every session.
- **Notes**: those you gave the session, and those it read, listed under the column.
- **The rest**: the harness's own instructions and the conversation.
- **Room**: what is left.

What you point to in your message stands in the box, as pointers.

What the agent dropped when it compacted its conversation can't be seen from here, and the view says so. It needs [Iso](/iso/iso.md "requires")'s pane to be showing a session.

On the Atlas, the context switch (Alt+C) marks what the session gave, read and changed.
