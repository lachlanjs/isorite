With your own agent attached, give it [your notes](/your-notes.md) and ask it to add a link to a guide note that suits them.

See its words marked in your notes, with provenance on. Then put the note back as it was, from its timeline.

This needs isorite of your own, not the guide's site: see [starting a base](/your-own-base/starting-a-base.md).
