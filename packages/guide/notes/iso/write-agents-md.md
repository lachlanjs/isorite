title: Write an AGENTS.md
uses: [iso/agents-md]
---

In a base of your own, write an AGENTS.md of three lines or so: your notation, your level, and one thing you want Iso to do or not do.

Then ask Iso something, and find it in the Context view.

This needs isorite of your own, not the guide's site: see [starting a base](/your-own-base/starting-a-base.md).
