uses: [iso/agents-md]

In a base of your own, write an AGENTS.md of three lines or so: your notation, your level, and one thing you want the agent to do or not do.

Then ask your agent something, and see whether it follows it.

This needs isorite of your own, not the guide's site: see [starting a base](/your-own-base/starting-a-base.md).
