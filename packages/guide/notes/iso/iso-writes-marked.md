---
type: Concept
title: Iso writes, marked
description: Iso's changes are made, marked as its own, and undone a turn at a time.
---

With the switch to edit notes on, [Iso](/iso/iso.md "requires") changes your notes when you ask it to, without asking you to accept each change first. It is safe because [every change is kept](/notes/every-change-is-kept.md "requires"):

- Each change it made is listed under its turn, with a link to the note.
- Iso's words are marked as its own, with provenance on (Alt+P), until you edit them.
- The turn's undo puts back every note it changed, at once.

Without the switch, Iso can still offer words: "insert as yours" puts them in the note as your own, since you chose them.

Iso does not put words in your notes you did not ask for, and suggests splitting or merging notes rather than doing it.
