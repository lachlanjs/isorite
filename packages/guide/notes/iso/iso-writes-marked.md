---
type: Concept
title: The agent writes, marked
description: An agent's changes are made, marked as its own, with what you asked, and put back from the timeline.
---

An agent attached to your base changes your notes when you ask it to, without asking you to accept each change first, whether it writes through isorite's tools or edits the file with its own. It is safe because [every change is kept](/notes/every-change-is-kept.md "requires"):

- Each change is entered in the note's history under the agent's name, with what you had asked it.
- Its words are marked as its own, with provenance on (Alt+P), until you edit them.
- The note's timeline puts back any version: a change undone is a change of its own.

An agent can also offer words rather than write them: "insert" puts them in the note as your own, since you chose them. And it can ask a question back, which you keep as a question note, or not.

isorite asks it not to put words in your notes you did not ask for, and to suggest splitting or merging notes rather than doing it.
