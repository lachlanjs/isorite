Resolve [why are links Markdown links?](/notes/why-markdown-links.md): write an answer in your own words in the question's note, and mark it resolved.

Then write a question of your own about a guide note you found unclear, and embed it in [your notes](/general/your-notes.md). Find it on the Atlas, beside the note it is about.
