title: The click-hold menu
tags: [atlas, interface]
description: Hold on a note, an island or open ground, and tags stand out to choose from.
---

Press and hold. What you hold on decides the tags that stand out; slide to one and let go. Let go anywhere else and nothing happens.

**On a note or an island**, four tags:

- **Move**, blue: pick it up and put it down in another island, or let go and it flies back.
- **Send**, gold: put it in your message to [the agent](/agents/the-agent-pane.md "see also").
- **Delete**, rose: you are asked first, and [every change is kept](/editing/every-change-is-kept.md "see also").
- **Hide**, grey: [take it off the map](/atlas/hide-and-unhide.md "see also") without deleting it.

An island has a fifth, a pencil, to rename it.

**On open ground**, two: a new note, or a new empty island: see [making islands and notes](/atlas/make-islands-and-notes.md "see also"). Once something is hidden, a third finds it.

On a phone, a long press does the same, and the browser's own menu and text selection stay out of the way.
