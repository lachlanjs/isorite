---
type: Concept
title: Hiding and unhiding
tags: [atlas, interface]
description: A hidden note or island is off the map and the list, but not gone.
---

Hold on a note or an island and choose the grey tag. It leaves the map and the note list, and the agent still finds it: hiding takes a thing out of your sight, not out of the base.

Once something is hidden, holding on open ground offers a third tag, to find it: type a few words, and choose the thing to show it again. Ctrl+K lists hidden notes too, last.

What is hidden is kept in the base's settings, so everyone with the base sees the same map. It holds in every [lens](/atlas/lenses.md "see also").
