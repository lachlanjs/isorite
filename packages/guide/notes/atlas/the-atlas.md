title: The Atlas
tags: [atlas, map]
description: The map of the base, drawn from what rests on what.
---

The Atlas is the base as a map. Each note is a block, each island a frame, and each [link](/notes/links-and-their-kinds.md "requires") a line. Notes are placed so that what a note requires stands before it, and the lines between islands run as trunks along the gaps.

- **Click a note** to choose it: it lights with its links, the notes it rests on and those that rest on it, and the rest dims. **Click it again** to open it. Click one it rests on to step back to it; click open ground to let go.
- **Click an island** to bring it to the screen, and again to step out. Islands open as you zoom in and close to one block as you zoom out.
- **Hold** on a note, an island or open ground for [the menu](/atlas/the-click-hold-menu.md "see also"): make, move, send, delete, hide.
- **Shift+click a note** gives it to [the agent](/agents/the-agent-pane.md "see also").
- **Ctrl+K** finds a note and outlines the matches as you type.

A link under the mouse, in a note or the agent's answer, is outlined on the map with its line lit. The layout changes as the base does, but starts from where it was, so the map you know moves as little as it can.

Open [questions and problems](/atlas/questions-and-problems.md "see also") are on the map beside the notes they are about. What it shows besides is chosen by [the switches](/general/the-switches.md "see also"), and it can group the same notes another way, by a [lens](/atlas/lenses.md "see also").
