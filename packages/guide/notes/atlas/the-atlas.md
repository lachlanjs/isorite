title: The Atlas
tags: [atlas, map]
description: The map of the base, drawn from what rests on what.
---

The Atlas is the base as a map. Each note is a block, each folder a frame, and each [link](/notes/links-and-their-kinds.md "requires") a line. Notes are placed so that what a note requires stands before it, along its folder's way, and the lines between folders run as trunks along the gaps.

- **Click a note** to choose it: it lights with its links, the notes it rests on and the notes that rest on it, and the rest dims. **Click it again** to open it in the Note pane. Click one it rests on to step back to it, the way kept lit; click open ground to let go.
- **Click a folder** to bring it to the screen, and again to step out to the folder it is in. Folders open as you zoom in on them and close to one block as you zoom out: see [folders](/notes/folders.md "see also").
- **Shift+click a note**, or hold on it, to give it to [the agent](/agent/agent.md "see also"). On a phone, a long press offers it.
- **Hold on open ground** and two tags stand out from the spot: slide to one and let go to make a new note there, or a new folder (with its first note). Let go anywhere else and nothing is made.
- **Ctrl+K** (⌘K) finds a note: type a few words and the notes that match are outlined on the map as you type, dashed for the words in them, dotted for what they mean, solid for both, and the rest step back. The arrows choose one, Enter opens it, Escape lets the map go. Hidden notes are listed too, last.
- **Hold on a note or a folder** and four tags stand out: move it, put it in your message to the agent, delete it, or hide it (the grey one). A folder has a fifth, a pencil, to rename it. A hidden note or folder is off the map and the note list, but not gone: agents still find it. Once something is hidden, a third tag, to find it, stands out when you hold on open ground: type a few words, and choose it to show it again. What is hidden is kept in the base's settings, so everyone with the base sees the same map.
- Drag the ground to move about, and pinch or scroll to zoom.

The note open in the Note pane has a bar inside its left edge. When it is off the screen, a pip at the Atlas's edge points to it: click the pip to bring it into view. A link under the mouse, in a note or in the agent's answer, is outlined on the map with its line lit, and a note under the mouse on the map marks the open note's links to it.

The layout changes as the base does, but starts from where it was, so the map you know moves as little as it can.

Open [questions](/questions-and-problems/questions.md "see also") and [problems](/questions-and-problems/problems.md "see also") are on the map too, each beside the notes it is about, and a solved problem shows it.

What it shows besides is chosen by [the switches](/atlas/the-switches.md "see also").
