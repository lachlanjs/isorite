title: Making islands and notes
tags: [atlas, islands]
description: An island is a folder drawn as a frame; a note or an empty island is made by holding on open ground.
---

A base is a folder of notes, and may hold folders, which the Atlas draws as islands, as this guide does: one each for General, Editing, Notes, the Atlas, Bases and Agents. An island is a frame, open when it is big enough on the screen to show its notes and one block when not. Links between islands are drawn between them, along the gaps.

To make a note, [hold](/atlas/the-click-hold-menu.md "uses") still on open ground inside the island it belongs in and slide to "new note": "Untitled note" is made where you held, open to type its title over. Slide to "new island" instead and an empty island is made there, framed on the map with its name chosen to type over. The list of notes makes one too, by the island button beside New note. A note moves by changing its island in its [details](/notes/a-notes-details.md "see also"), or by the move tag.

The islands are folders on disk, so moving a note on the Atlas moves its file and the links to it follow. An empty island is an empty folder, with nothing written in it to hold its place. It stays through a reload and waits for notes, but git does not keep an empty folder, so a clone of the base lacks it until a note is put in it.
