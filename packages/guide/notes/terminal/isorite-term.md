---
type: Concept
title: isorite term
description: An agent in the terminal, with your notes as its tools, followed in the browser.
---

`isorite term <base>`, run in a project's folder, starts OpenCode, an agent in the terminal, with your base as tools of its own: it reads your notes before it writes code, and changes them where the code makes them wrong.

The browser follows it. In Iso's pane, with the project's files or commands switched on, the terminal's conversation is shown as it goes, and can be answered from there, on a laptop or a phone. Its changes to notes are marked as the agent's, as [Iso's are](/iso/iso-writes-marked.md "uses").

It is given the base's [AGENTS.md](/iso/agents-md.md "uses") too, and what isorite tells Iso about a base.

Setting it up, with a provider or a gateway of your own, is in the guide on GitHub: [the terminal](https://github.com/lachlanjs/isorite/blob/main/docs/terminal.md).
