---
type: Concept
title: Every change is kept
tags: [editing, history]
description: Every change to a note is kept, with who made it, and an agent's words are marked as its own.
---

isorite keeps every change to every note in a journal, in `.isorite/`, with who made it (you, or an agent) and what you had asked it. Two switches in the Note pane show it:

- **The timeline** (Alt+T): the note's history as layers in its margin, newest on top, each as thick as the change: yours blue, an agent's gold. Click one to see the note as it was, and restore it if you want it back. Restoring is itself a change, so nothing is lost.
- **Provenance** (Alt+P): whose words are whose. Words you wrote are plain; words an agent wrote, and you have not touched, are marked. Edit them and they become yours.

That is why an agent may write without asking you to accept each change, whether through isorite's tools or by editing the file itself. It is asked not to put in words you did not ask for. It can also offer words rather than write them (they go in as your own, since you chose them), or ask a [question](/atlas/questions-and-problems.md "see also") back.
