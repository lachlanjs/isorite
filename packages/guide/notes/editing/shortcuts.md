Ctrl is ⌘ on a Mac.

**Writing**

- `[[` and a few letters of a title: link a note. Alt+1, 2 or 3 sets the link's kind: [requires, uses, see also](/notes/links-and-their-kinds.md "uses").
- Ctrl+click a link: follow it.
- `[[!` and a name: put a picture, a question or a problem in the note.
- Ctrl+[ and Ctrl+]: point at the selected passage in your message to [the agent](/agents/give-it-notes.md "see also").

**Finding**

- Ctrl+K: find a note.

**Switches**

- The note: Alt+P provenance, Alt+T timeline.
- The Atlas: Alt+Q questions, Alt+B problems, Alt+W web links, Alt+K code, Alt+I files, Alt+C the agent's context.
- Agent: Alt+N may it edit the notes, Alt+F the project's files, Alt+R run commands.

What each does is in [the switches](/general/the-switches.md "requires").
