type: Problem
title: Give it notes
tags: [agents, practice, context]
uses: [agents/the-agent-pane]
---

Give the agent a note from the Atlas (shift+click it, hold on it, or drag it to the Agent pane). It stands in your message as a pointer: press it to open the note, or delete it to take it out.

Then point at a passage. Select some words in a note and press Ctrl+[: a small blue pointer stands in your message. Ask "what does this mean" with the pointer for "this", and the agent is sent the note, the lines and the words themselves. Ctrl+] copies the same for another place.
