In a base of your own, write an AGENTS.md of three lines: your notation, your level, and one thing the agent should or should not do.

Then give your agent [your notes](/general/your-notes.md) and ask it to add a link to a guide note that suits them. See whether it kept to your three lines, and see its words marked, with provenance on. Put the note back as it was, from its timeline.

This needs isorite of your own, not the guide's site: see [starting a base](/bases/starting-a-base.md).
