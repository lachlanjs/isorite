isorite is a home for what you are coming to understand: notes in Markdown, a map of what rests on what, and an agent that reads and writes them with you. Every change to a note is kept, with who made it, so your understanding keeps pace with what the agent does.

This guide is a base like any other, and its subject is isorite. It is in six islands: General, Editing, Notes, Atlas, Bases and Agents. Pick the Tour lens in the Atlas's header to read it in order, a step at a time, each ending in something to work out.
