What isorite asks of a base, and what [the agent](/agents/the-agent-pane.md "uses") is told to keep to:

- **One idea a note**, with a title that names the idea and a description that says it in a line.
- **Look before you write.** If a note says it already, extend that note.
- **Link by what it rests on**: "requires" for what must be understood first, "uses" for what it draws on. See [links and their kinds](/notes/links-and-their-kinds.md "requires").
- **In your own words.** The agent puts no words in your notes you did not ask for, and its own stay marked until you make them yours.
- **Leave an open question open.** It marks where your understanding stops: see [questions](/atlas/questions-and-problems.md "uses").
- **Check, do not solve.** A problem is yours to solve; the agent checks your solution.

A note that holds two ideas, or two notes that hold one, are yours to split or merge. Habits you want in every base, such as how to name a note, belong in the global base: see [writing notes](global:conventions/writing-notes.md "see also") for the one this site keeps as an example.
