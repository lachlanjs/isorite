The base's name before the first pane's name is the switcher. Press it for the bases isorite holds: [the global base](/bases/the-global-base.md "see also") and [the skills base](/bases/the-skills-base.md "see also") first, then your own. Choose one and it opens in this tab; two tabs show two bases side by side. In your own isorite you can rename a base (its address stays) and [lend it to the agent](/bases/memory.md "see also") from the list.

The site you are reading holds four, fixed, as examples:

- **Tutorial**: this guide.
- **Code**: a small command-line program, its code shown beside the notes that explain it, with lenses of its own.
- **Skills**: a person's skills as islands, with the skill isorite ships beside them.
- **Global**: knowledge that holds across bases. The notes in the others link to it: the mark beside a note opens it.

A change you make in one is kept in this browser, apart from the others. Reset guide, in the Note pane's settings, starts only the base that is open again.

Each base has its own [lenses](/atlas/lenses.md "see also") and journal.
