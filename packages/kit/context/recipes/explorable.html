Why a fast wheel turns backwards

Sampled too slowly, a signal is indistinguishable from a slower one. Which one, and when?

The signal and the sampling

One second the signal, its samples, and the slower wave through them

Gold dots are the samples. Above the Nyquist limit the blue wave, slower, passes through every one of them as well as the true signal does: from the samples alone, the two can't be told apart.
