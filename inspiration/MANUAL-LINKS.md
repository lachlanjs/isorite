# Links to open by hand

Images I couldn't download or couldn't find good ones of. Save anything you like into the folder named beside it. I
haven't checked what each search returns today; the Wikimedia Commons ones are searches, so they always load.

## Alien: Isolation (`alien-isolation/`)

The fan wiki is blocked for you, so these are other routes to the map and inventory screens:

- https://steamcommunity.com/app/214490/screenshots/ : players' screenshots. Filter by "Most popular"; the map and the
  item wheel turn up often.
- https://www.gameuidatabase.com/ : search "Alien: Isolation". It refused me (403), but it may load for you. It
  catalogues menus, maps, inventories and HUDs screen by screen.
- https://fontsinuse.com/uses/77513/alien-isolation-video-game : where the screenshots here came from; it also shows
  the typefaces (Helvetica and the pixel face Jixellation).
- Worth finding: the **map** zoomed in on one room with its icons, the **motion tracker** close up, the **save
  terminal** (the "registering" screen), and the **access tuner / rewire** screens, which are the game's best small
  interfaces.

## Minerals (`minerals/`)

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=gold+in+quartz&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image : a gold vein in
  quartz, the image for "notes joined by veins". The searches I ran for veins returned landscapes.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=bismuth+hopper+crystal&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image : more
  bismuth: stepped habit and iridescent lustre in one.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=pyrite+lustre&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image : metallic
  lustre at its clearest.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=lapis+lazuli+polished&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image : your
  lapis ground, as the stone.

## Organic growth (`organic-growth/`)

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=Lindenmayer+system&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image : L-system
  drawings (my searches found none large enough).
- https://algorithmicbotany.org/papers/ : *The Algorithmic Beauty of Plants* (Prusinkiewicz and Lindenmayer), free
  as PDF from the authors. The plates are the reference for L-systems.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=leaf+venation&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image : leaf veins,
  thick to thin.

## Prospective (`prospective/`)

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=William+Smith+geological+map+1815&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image :
  the first national geological map. Probably the single best image for the "geological survey" theme.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=Michel-L%C3%A9vy+chart&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image : the
  interference-colour chart for thin sections.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=mineral+collection+drawer&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image :
  specimen drawers and labels (mine returned archive photos).
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=Physarum+Tokyo&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image : the slime
  mould that grew the Tokyo rail network.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=commutative+diagram&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image :
  isomorphism, drawn.
