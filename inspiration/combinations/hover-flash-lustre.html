Where it's used

  • The three panes' headers (Note, Atlas, Iso). The name in Departure Mono at 15 px, then what the pane holds now in small mono: the note's folder and title, the base and its size, Iso's tier and spend. The pane's own controls stay at the right, as now.
  • Which pane you're in is the one solid bar; the others are outline only. The Note and Atlas bars are lattice blue, Iso's is gold, so the accent says who the pane belongs to (blue for structure and you, gold for Iso), as the tokens already do.
  • The key legend sits at the foot of the pane you're in only, and only on a keyboard (it isn't shown on a phone or with touch). It names the keys that work in that pane now, not every key the app has.
  • Dialogs (Note settings, Iso settings, the provider setup) get the same bar, always solid, so they read as screens of the same machine.
  • On a phone, one pane at a time is shown, so its bar is always solid.

Gray areas

  • The app may not track "the pane you're in" today. If it doesn't, the solid bar needs a rule: the last pane clicked or focused.
  • A solid gold bar on Iso while it's answering might be the better rule than focus: Iso's bar fills while it works and empties when done. That would make the bar a status light, not a focus marker. Pick one; both at once would be confusing.
  • The legend's keys here are invented to show the format. The real ones come from the app.
